## Sprint 8 · Nov 9 – Nov 15, 2026

### What shipped

#### fieldwire_client_ucsh
33 done · 294 points · 36 pull requests merged (roney4719)

- **The app now works the way Fieldwire web does.** After an audit of every workflow against Fieldwire web, each function sits where Fieldwire puts it and uses Fieldwire's names. Home is the one projects screen, with projects and templates tabs. The project name switches projects, the door screen is a single board with tick boxes and shift-click ranges, notes became comments, and form templates are edited once for the account. ([#1196](https://github.com/Upper-Canada-Specialty-Hardware/fieldwire_client_ucsh/issues/1196))
- **Layout follow-ups from real use.** Reports and history are back in the sidebar list. Door cards show checklist progress again, with more room between them. Sort offers what Fieldwire offers, filters are a row of dropdowns, and plans zoom faster with the mouse wheel. The account's form templates read as a library that projects add from. ([#1235](https://github.com/Upper-Canada-Specialty-Hardware/fieldwire_client_ucsh/issues/1235))
- **Mirrors, one per GC, on their own page.** A project can now have several mirrors, one per general contractor, each named for it and managed from a Mirrors page in the project. A failed mirror can be tried again in place, a stopped one resumed, and sync waits while a set up or update runs on the real project instead of copying mid-update. ([#1202](https://github.com/Upper-Canada-Specialty-Hardware/fieldwire_client_ucsh/issues/1202))
- **Mirrors build reliably and show their progress.** A new mirror stays hidden and shows its build stages in the activity panel until its first sync lands, instead of reading active while empty. Set up now waits for the mirror's template to fill, retrying on a growing schedule rather than failing at a fixed limit, so statuses, categories and tags copy. Mirrors are built as the UCSH API account, which fixed a refused placeholder door and a failed member check. ([#1169](https://github.com/Upper-Canada-Specialty-Hardware/fieldwire_client_ucsh/issues/1169), [#1197](https://github.com/Upper-Canada-Specialty-Hardware/fieldwire_client_ucsh/issues/1197), [#1168](https://github.com/Upper-Canada-Specialty-Hardware/fieldwire_client_ucsh/issues/1168), [#1247](https://github.com/Upper-Canada-Specialty-Hardware/fieldwire_client_ucsh/issues/1247))
- **Simpler mirror options and office-hours sync.** The new mirror dialog went from 17 checkboxes, two of which copied nothing, to eight options named the way Fieldwire names them. Mirrors now sync once an hour from 7 am to 6 pm on weekdays instead of every 5 minutes all day, with a sync now button for anything outside those hours. ([#1258](https://github.com/Upper-Canada-Specialty-Hardware/fieldwire_client_ucsh/issues/1258), [#1358](https://github.com/Upper-Canada-Specialty-Hardware/fieldwire_client_ucsh/issues/1358))
- **Draw and edit markups on plans.** People can draw pen, arrow and text markups on a plan, then move, change or delete them with undo, and the markups appear in Fieldwire web and mobile. Only your own markups can be changed; project admins can delete anyone's. ([#1228](https://github.com/Upper-Canada-Specialty-Hardware/fieldwire_client_ucsh/issues/1228))
- **Web pages and sign-in on our own domain.** The access service and its sign-in, review and email links moved from Railway addresses onto ucsh.cloud, and sign-in moved to a production Clerk instance on that domain. ([#809](https://github.com/Upper-Canada-Specialty-Hardware/fieldwire_client_ucsh/issues/809))
- **Stay signed in for 30 days.** One sign-in now lasts 30 days, and each renewal re-reads the person from Clerk, so a role change reaches the app within 15 minutes and a removed person is signed out. ([#1119](https://github.com/Upper-Canada-Specialty-Hardware/fieldwire_client_ucsh/issues/1119))
- **Trash and project actions.** Like Fieldwire, a project's settings now offer leave, archive and delete, and a trash tab restores doors, plans, files, folders and forms deleted in the last 30 days. Only a project admin can archive or delete a project. ([#1185](https://github.com/Upper-Canada-Specialty-Hardware/fieldwire_client_ucsh/issues/1185))
- **Door spreadsheet export in the report builder.** The doors spreadsheet came back inside the report builder with a live preview, a layout grouped by door with its checklist items under it, a note of what it is filtered by, and saved templates to start from. The Excel preview now looks like the spreadsheet it makes, with column letters, row numbers and grid lines. ([#1265](https://github.com/Upper-Canada-Specialty-Hardware/fieldwire_client_ucsh/issues/1265), [#1314](https://github.com/Upper-Canada-Specialty-Hardware/fieldwire_client_ucsh/issues/1314))
- **Faster PDF report preview, and print to paper.** The preview keeps its window and pictures between redraws, so a text-only change downloads and draws nothing, and page 1 shows first. Reports can now print straight to a printer, and shift-click anywhere on a door card selects it instead of opening it. ([#1316](https://github.com/Upper-Canada-Specialty-Hardware/fieldwire_client_ucsh/issues/1316))
- **Smoother app on every monitor.** The app follows each monitor's refresh rate, filters the door list off the screen thread and stops animating layout. A first attempt to pin screens to their own cores made scrolling worse, so only the backend is now kept off two fast cores. ([#1297](https://github.com/Upper-Canada-Specialty-Hardware/fieldwire_client_ucsh/issues/1297))
- **Screens open with their data already there.** At launch the app loads the person's own projects in the background and keeps them on disk, then syncs only changes every 30 seconds while a project is open. ([#1298](https://github.com/Upper-Canada-Specialty-Hardware/fieldwire_client_ucsh/issues/1298))
- **Checklist ticks change on the click.** A tick shows at once and saves behind it; only a failed save rolls the item back, with a message saying why. Closing a window waits for unsaved answers. ([#1346](https://github.com/Upper-Canada-Specialty-Hardware/fieldwire_client_ucsh/issues/1346))
- **Project pages in their own windows.** Any project page can pop out into a full window with the sidebar, ticked projects on home open in their own windows, and each window shows its number. ([#1266](https://github.com/Upper-Canada-Specialty-Hardware/fieldwire_client_ucsh/issues/1266))
- **New project picks a template.** The new project dialog offers the account's templates and waits for the chosen one to copy in before opening the project, so a set up run right after no longer stops on missing parts. ([#651](https://github.com/Upper-Canada-Specialty-Hardware/fieldwire_client_ucsh/issues/651))
- **Duplicated doors in the update review.** The review now lists each case's duplicate doors with links to open them in Fieldwire or the app, and a check again button, so the person decides which to keep instead of the page guessing. ([#1155](https://github.com/Upper-Canada-Specialty-Hardware/fieldwire_client_ucsh/issues/1155))
- **No more empty doors from updates.** An opening the schedule gives no checklist lines is no longer created by an update or set up; it is listed with its reason instead. At South Niagara two such doors had come back on every run. ([#1167](https://github.com/Upper-Canada-Specialty-Hardware/fieldwire_client_ucsh/issues/1167))
- **Clearer door cards and sidebar.** Door cards show the category as a pin when the door is on a plan and a square when not, and the progress bar stretches with the card. Select by list is its own toolbar button, and mirrors became a sidebar row. ([#1251](https://github.com/Upper-Canada-Specialty-Hardware/fieldwire_client_ucsh/issues/1251), [#1313](https://github.com/Upper-Canada-Specialty-Hardware/fieldwire_client_ucsh/issues/1313))
- **Sign-in screen fixes.** The screen now says it creates a first-time person's account, code-limit errors say to wait, and a downloaded update can be installed from the sign-in screen. The sign-in animation is now smooth vector art. ([#1284](https://github.com/Upper-Canada-Specialty-Hardware/fieldwire_client_ucsh/issues/1284), [#1289](https://github.com/Upper-Canada-Specialty-Hardware/fieldwire_client_ucsh/issues/1289), [#1351](https://github.com/Upper-Canada-Specialty-Hardware/fieldwire_client_ucsh/issues/1351))
- **Fewer emails and a tidier menu.** Adding someone to a project sends only Fieldwire's invite, projects the app creates start with task and plan emails off, and the account menu keeps only region and sign out. ([#1143](https://github.com/Upper-Canada-Specialty-Hardware/fieldwire_client_ucsh/issues/1143), [#1149](https://github.com/Upper-Canada-Specialty-Hardware/fieldwire_client_ucsh/issues/1149), [#1114](https://github.com/Upper-Canada-Specialty-Hardware/fieldwire_client_ucsh/issues/1114))
- **Smaller fixes.** The pin on report plan pictures is a teardrop that leaves the door number readable, and the access service's cleanup sweep no longer fails every 30 seconds. ([#1317](https://github.com/Upper-Canada-Specialty-Hardware/fieldwire_client_ucsh/issues/1317), [#1327](https://github.com/Upper-Canada-Specialty-Hardware/fieldwire_client_ucsh/issues/1327))
- **Releases.** 30 releases went out, v3.0.240 to v3.0.269.

Also merged, still being verified: a create account screen with real sign-in error messages ([#1290](https://github.com/Upper-Canada-Specialty-Hardware/fieldwire_client_ucsh/issues/1290)), and naming a door with no number after the room it opens into ([#1156](https://github.com/Upper-Canada-Specialty-Hardware/fieldwire_client_ucsh/issues/1156)).

#### uc-nexus
25 done · 184 points · 24 pull requests merged (Xilous)

- **The Nexus guide bot.** The help bubble gained an ask mode: a person asks how anything in Nexus works in their own words, and the guide answers from a guidebook of every screen and points to the control on their screen. It can walk them there a tap at a time, or work the screen itself when asked, and never saves anything. ([#2322](https://github.com/Upper-Canada-Specialty-Hardware/uc-nexus/issues/2322))
- **Guide bot refined from the owner's tests.** The guide now explains in words with numbered steps, and each answer comes with its walkthrough, a button per way. A step card travels to each control with a highlight instead of an arrow. The guide bot's window floats beside dialogs and moves with its bubble. ([#2351](https://github.com/Upper-Canada-Specialty-Hardware/uc-nexus/issues/2351), [#2369](https://github.com/Upper-Canada-Specialty-Hardware/uc-nexus/issues/2369))
- **Long guide answers read properly.** A long answer opens at its start rather than its end, and its bold, headings and lists show as formatting instead of raw asterisks. ([#2384](https://github.com/Upper-Canada-Specialty-Hardware/uc-nexus/issues/2384))
- **Custom items are inventory.** Each custom item type has its own fields, each item its own values and what is on hand and where. A warehouse manager can add stock with no PO, at its cost, and the values show wherever an item is picked or shipped. ([#2349](https://github.com/Upper-Canada-Specialty-Hardware/uc-nexus/issues/2349))
- **Edit a PO's lines after GP registration.** Order as, ordered quantity and unit cost stay editable on a registered PO until its first GP registered receive. Quantity and cost changes are written into the PO in GP, with tax and job cost following, and a save GP misses waits in the queue. ([#2396](https://github.com/Upper-Canada-Specialty-Hardware/uc-nexus/issues/2396))
- **Any GP purchase tax, or none.** Register PO now lets a PO user tick any GP purchase tax details, such as BC GST and PST together, or pick no tax, instead of only tax schedules. The office had been adding these by hand in GP. ([#2410](https://github.com/Upper-Canada-Specialty-Hardware/uc-nexus/issues/2410))
- **Users in several companies.** Being in a company is now apart from holding roles in it: a UC Nexus admin adds an account to a company, and that company's tenant owner gives the roles. Each wait has its own screen that checks again by itself. User management uses one access grid, with a column per GP company, which works even with the relay down or in demo mode. ([#2229](https://github.com/Upper-Canada-Specialty-Hardware/uc-nexus/issues/2229), [#2389](https://github.com/Upper-Canada-Specialty-Hardware/uc-nexus/issues/2389))
- **Put-away alerts for approved receives.** An approved physical receive now pops up on every warehouse user's screen until the delivery is put away or they close it, so nothing sits on the floor unnoticed. ([#2230](https://github.com/Upper-Canada-Specialty-Hardware/uc-nexus/issues/2230))
- **Shipping settings and shipment notes.** Shipping has a settings page per tenant for the site form's division address, its defaults and the shipment methods. A shipment can carry notes that print on its site form above the sign-off. ([#2285](https://github.com/Upper-Canada-Specialty-Hardware/uc-nexus/issues/2285), [#2303](https://github.com/Upper-Canada-Specialty-Hardware/uc-nexus/issues/2303))
- **Floating inventory redefined.** Floating inventory is now hardware no opening asks for, wherever it sits, such as screwdrivers and bolts, and a project with no schedule can request and ship it as it is. ([#2302](https://github.com/Upper-Canada-Specialty-Hardware/uc-nexus/issues/2302))
- **Receiving clean-up.** Leftovers from the earlier receiving rework were removed and the GP registered receives page now pages on the server, with no change in behaviour. ([#2265](https://github.com/Upper-Canada-Specialty-Hardware/uc-nexus/issues/2265))
- **By others is back at schedule upload.** A product new to a schedule can be set to by others at upload, and on the classifications tab while nothing is on it yet. ([#2286](https://github.com/Upper-Canada-Specialty-Hardware/uc-nexus/issues/2286))
- **PO document addresses.** The PO's top-left block is labelled Bill To, and each company keeps a list of ship-to addresses, each with its own tax numbers and bullets, picked on the PO. The picked address now actually prints, and the reference goes before the item number. ([#2404](https://github.com/Upper-Canada-Specialty-Hardware/uc-nexus/issues/2404), [#2426](https://github.com/Upper-Canada-Specialty-Hardware/uc-nexus/issues/2426), [#2414](https://github.com/Upper-Canada-Specialty-Hardware/uc-nexus/issues/2414), [#2435](https://github.com/Upper-Canada-Specialty-Hardware/uc-nexus/issues/2435))
- **PO fixes.** New UCSH POs raised in GP show in Nexus again, after a legacy short PO number had stalled the check. A GP-born PO on a specialties project registers each line by a typed name, and the register drops its origin toggle. ([#2394](https://github.com/Upper-Canada-Specialty-Hardware/uc-nexus/issues/2394), [#2280](https://github.com/Upper-Canada-Specialty-Hardware/uc-nexus/issues/2280), [#2380](https://github.com/Upper-Canada-Specialty-Hardware/uc-nexus/issues/2380))
- **Stale tabs reload themselves.** A tab left open across a deploy now reloads by itself, which was why a site form failed to generate, and the site form now says why it failed. ([#2304](https://github.com/Upper-Canada-Specialty-Hardware/uc-nexus/issues/2304))
- **Upkeep.** A flaky shipment methods test that could skip a backend deploy was fixed, along with the same race in the app. A legacy UC Connects receiving problem was looked into and not pursued. ([#2407](https://github.com/Upper-Canada-Specialty-Hardware/uc-nexus/issues/2407), [#2281](https://github.com/Upper-Canada-Specialty-Hardware/uc-nexus/issues/2281))

Closed without shipping: [#2434](https://github.com/Upper-Canada-Specialty-Hardware/uc-nexus/issues/2434), [#2427](https://github.com/Upper-Canada-Specialty-Hardware/uc-nexus/issues/2427) and [#2419](https://github.com/Upper-Canada-Specialty-Hardware/uc-nexus/issues/2419).
