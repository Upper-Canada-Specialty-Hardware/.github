### What shipped

#### uc-nexus
30 done · 243 points · 30 pull requests merged (Xilous) · 3 releases, relay-v0.1.0-build.97 to relay-v0.1.0-build.99

- **Several schedules per GP job.** One GP job can now carry several hardware schedules, one per building or phase, each revised on its own, and its POs, receipts and stock now belong to a schedule. ([#2139](https://github.com/Upper-Canada-Specialty-Hardware/uc-nexus/issues/2139))
- **GP writes settle themselves.** Nexus now settles a GP write it can't confirm by checking GP itself, instead of asking users to check or retry. ([#1971](https://github.com/Upper-Canada-Specialty-Hardware/uc-nexus/issues/1971))
- **One user, several companies.** A PO user can now hold roles and a GP buyer identity in both UCSH and UBC, and switch between them. ([#2005](https://github.com/Upper-Canada-Specialty-Hardware/uc-nexus/issues/2005))
- **Bug bash rulings.** The product owner's bug bash rulings are built in, such as stock allocated to a job no longer reading as not purchased. ([#1966](https://github.com/Upper-Canada-Specialty-Hardware/uc-nexus/issues/1966))
- **Backend stalls fixed.** One slow sign-in or storage call no longer holds up every other request. ([#1968](https://github.com/Upper-Canada-Specialty-Hardware/uc-nexus/issues/1968))
- **Re-link off-schedule inventory.** Off-schedule inventory can now be re-linked onto the schedule in one step, and the grid shows each row's schedule status. ([#1996](https://github.com/Upper-Canada-Specialty-Hardware/uc-nexus/issues/1996), [#2118](https://github.com/Upper-Canada-Specialty-Hardware/uc-nexus/issues/2118))
- **Inventory filters.** Warehouse users can filter by PO and job number, without dropped keystrokes when typing fast or scanning. ([#2086](https://github.com/Upper-Canada-Specialty-Hardware/uc-nexus/issues/2086), [#2170](https://github.com/Upper-Canada-Specialty-Hardware/uc-nexus/issues/2170))
- **Product codes capped.** Codes over 100 characters, usually notes in the wrong field, are now refused everywhere. ([#2111](https://github.com/Upper-Canada-Specialty-Hardware/uc-nexus/issues/2111))
- **UBC go-live sweep.** Every workflow UBC staff will use was tested end to end in production, the small problems found were fixed, and UBC's 1,781 warehouse locations are registered. ([#2127](https://github.com/Upper-Canada-Specialty-Hardware/uc-nexus/issues/2127), [#2149](https://github.com/Upper-Canada-Specialty-Hardware/uc-nexus/issues/2149), [#2104](https://github.com/Upper-Canada-Specialty-Hardware/uc-nexus/issues/2104))
- **Reviewed relay builds only.** Only a reviewed merge to master can now ship a relay build, and the relay checks its hash before installing. ([#1967](https://github.com/Upper-Canada-Specialty-Hardware/uc-nexus/issues/1967))
- **POs gone from GP.** A PO that disappears from GP now says so instead of reading as cancelled. ([#1970](https://github.com/Upper-Canada-Specialty-Hardware/uc-nexus/issues/1970))
- **USD POs register.** USD POs, refused on most days since July, now register using the exchange rate GP would pick. ([#1972](https://github.com/Upper-Canada-Specialty-Hardware/uc-nexus/issues/1972))
- **UBC legacy-list import.** Specialties jobs land whole on their projects, 8,775 units across 21 jobs that would have gone to the pool; after one reversal, other rows go to the pool, and inactive jobs, BC warehouse stock and retail orders stay out. ([#2042](https://github.com/Upper-Canada-Specialty-Hardware/uc-nexus/issues/2042), [#2096](https://github.com/Upper-Canada-Specialty-Hardware/uc-nexus/issues/2096), [#2100](https://github.com/Upper-Canada-Specialty-Hardware/uc-nexus/issues/2100), [#2106](https://github.com/Upper-Canada-Specialty-Hardware/uc-nexus/issues/2106))
- **Receiving.** Staff can note a single received line, such as "box crushed", and file uploads, whose failure had blocked every receive, work again. ([#2085](https://github.com/Upper-Canada-Specialty-Hardware/uc-nexus/issues/2085), [#1987](https://github.com/Upper-Canada-Specialty-Hardware/uc-nexus/issues/1987))
- **Item types.** A custom item can change type, taking its stock along, and type codes now clash only within a company. ([#2110](https://github.com/Upper-Canada-Specialty-Hardware/uc-nexus/issues/2110), [#2043](https://github.com/Upper-Canada-Specialty-Hardware/uc-nexus/issues/2043))
- **Upkeep.** CI meets the org standard, the frontend runs on Node 26, and the docs mark the UBC–UCSH merge postponed. ([#1981](https://github.com/Upper-Canada-Specialty-Hardware/uc-nexus/issues/1981), [#2074](https://github.com/Upper-Canada-Specialty-Hardware/uc-nexus/issues/2074), [#2080](https://github.com/Upper-Canada-Specialty-Hardware/uc-nexus/issues/2080), [#1993](https://github.com/Upper-Canada-Specialty-Hardware/uc-nexus/issues/1993), [#2044](https://github.com/Upper-Canada-Specialty-Hardware/uc-nexus/issues/2044), [#2173](https://github.com/Upper-Canada-Specialty-Hardware/uc-nexus/issues/2173))
